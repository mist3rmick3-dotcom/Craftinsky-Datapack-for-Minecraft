CraftinSky — Skyblock datapack that restores missing resources for progression.

1. Hoes on short grass
   - Use a hoe on short grass to obtain special seeds or resources depending on the hoe material:
     - Wooden: beetroot seeds
     - Stone: sweet berries
     - Copper: melon seeds
     - Iron: pumpkin seeds
     - Golden: sugar cane
     - Diamond: bamboo
     - Netherite: cactus

2. Glow Berries
   - Right-click a fully grown sweet berry bush while holding glowstone dust to convert the berries into glow berries (the glowstone dust is consumed).

3. Mushrooms
   - Place mud blocks around a composter (at least one block must touch the composter). When the composter is filled, it will fertilize the mud and produce mushrooms (up to 8 per conversion).

4. Strays
   - Strays can spawn naturally. When killed by a player, they have a 12.5% chance to drop snowballs in addition to their normal drops.

5. Husks
   - Husks can spawn naturally. When killed by a player, they have a 12.5% chance to drop sand in addition to their normal drops.

6. Coarse Dirt Conversion
   - Throw coarse dirt into water. After a short delay, it will transform into dirt with bubble particles, splash and level-up sounds, and happy villager effects. Only one transformation timer is active per item, so effects are never duplicated.

Other features:
- Main datapack functions are loaded automatically; no manual setup is required after installing the datapack.
- For more details, see the data/craftinsky/function/ folder.
